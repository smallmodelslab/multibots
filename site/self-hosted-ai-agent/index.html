Multibots

How do I run an AI agent on my own computer, around the clock?

Install Multibots on a computer that stays on, such as a Mac mini or a Linux desktop, and sign in to ChatGPT or connect a model you host. Each bot you make is an AI agent with its own conversation, files and shell on your computers, and routines that wake it on a schedule or when a webhook is called, so it keeps working while you're away. Your laptop reaches it directly over Tailscale. Multibots is free and open source, for Apple silicon Macs and 64-bit Linux, and your bots run on no server of ours.

What runs where

Every computer running Multibots is a server unless you turn that off: it keeps a copy of every bot, with its conversation, routines and files, and runs them. Packages a bot installs and files over 200 MB stay on the server that has them. A computer with server mode off only shows the bots. Your computers find each other over Tailscale and copy bots between them directly.

A bot's work leaves them as what it sends its model, which is ChatGPT or a model server you choose, what it reaches through the apps and tools you give it, and its webhooks' calls, which come in through our relay at hooks.wrkrsh.com. The privacy page lists everything that leaves them, usage analytics and update checks included.

Keep it working while you're away

Routines wake a bot once, weekly at a set time, every 15 minutes or more within the hours and days you pick, or whenever its webhook is called, by GitHub, Stripe, Slack or any sender with a key. Ask a bot to do something regularly and it sets the routine itself.

Bots run while at least one of your servers is on. A laptop that sleeps hands its bots to your other servers, which go on from each run's last saved step. Each server signs in to ChatGPT or adds its models for itself, so a desktop that stays on, with its own, keeps them working.

What a bot can do

  • Run any command in a shell on its computer, as you.
  • Search the web, when its run starts on ChatGPT.
  • See and use the screen through Multidesktop, and reach your other computers once you sign in to Multidesktop.
  • Use tabs of its own in your Chrome, through its extension.
  • Use Gmail, GitHub, Slack and hundreds more apps through Composio, on your own Composio key.
  • Talk with you in a voice chat, and draw with you on a whiteboard.

Before you start

A bot acts as you. Its shell runs any command with your permissions, unsandboxed and without asking, and anything it reads, such as a web page, an email or a webhook's call, can try to steer it. Make servers only of computers you would let it use, and give it only the apps you would let it act in. Then follow the install guide.
