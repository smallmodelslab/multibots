Multibots

Is there an open-source alternative to Grok Bot?

Yes: Multibots, free under the AGPL-3.0. Like Grok Bot, it gives each bot a conversation, routines on a schedule or webhook, its files and a computer to work on. Unlike Grok Bot, your bots live on your own Macs and Linux computers and run on your ChatGPT subscription or models you host, where Grok Bot runs them on a cloud computer and needs a Cursor account with a plan that includes it, or its trial. Multibots can bring your Grok Bot bots over.

When Grok Bot fits better

If you want bots that keep working while every computer you own is off, or on Windows or your phone, without keeping a computer of your own for them or minding what a shell can do on it, Grok Bot is the simpler choice.

When Multibots fits better

If you want your bots and their files kept on your computers, their contents going only to the models and apps a bot uses as it works with them (see privacy), run on the ChatGPT plan you already have or on models you host, and be free software you can read and change, Multibots fits. Start with the install guide.

Bring your Grok Bot bots over

New bot can copy a bot's name and instructions from the Grok Bot app on your computer, but not what it remembers, or start from a Grok Bot template recipe, a JSON file your Grok bot makes when you ask, which brings its memory too. A recipe's skills and work log become files in the bot's folder, and its first run asks the bot to set its routines up again, paused, and to ask you whether to turn them on. Check each one's schedule and whether it's on before you switch over; the shortest Multibots runs is every 15 minutes.
