Multibots

Is there an open-source alternative to Grok Bot?

Yes: Multibots, free under the AGPL-3.0. Like Grok Bot, it gives each bot a conversation, routines on a schedule or webhook, its files and a computer to work on. Unlike Grok Bot, your bots live on your own Macs and Linux computers and run on your ChatGPT subscription or models you host, where Grok Bot runs them on a cloud computer and needs a Cursor account with a plan that includes it. Multibots can bring your Grok Bot bots over.

How they differ

When Grok Bot fits better

If you want bots that keep working while every computer you own is off, or on Windows or your phone, without keeping a computer of your own for them or minding what a shell can do on it, Grok Bot is the simpler choice.

When Multibots fits better

If you want your bots and their files to stay on your computers, run on the ChatGPT plan you already have or on models you host, and be free software you can read and change, Multibots fits. Start with the install guide.

Bring your Grok Bot bots over

New bot can start from a bot the Grok Bot app keeps on your computer, with its name, instructions and colour, or from a Grok Bot template recipe, a JSON file your Grok bot makes when you ask. A recipe's skills and work log become files in the bot's folder, and on its first run the bot sets up the recipe's routines, paused until you turn them on.
