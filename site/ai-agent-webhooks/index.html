Multibots

How do I trigger an AI agent with a webhook or on a schedule?

Ask a bot in Multibots to do something regularly, or whenever something happens elsewhere, and it sets itself a routine that wakes it at a set time, once or weekly, every 15 minutes to 24 hours, or whenever its webhook is called. GitHub, Stripe, Slack, senders of Standard Webhooks or anything that sends a Bearer key can call that webhook at our relay, hooks.wrkrsh.com, which holds the calls it accepts for up to seven days until your computers take them, so a bot on your own computers gets its calls with no port open to the internet. Multibots is free and open source, for Apple silicon Macs and x86-64 Linux.

Ask for a routine

Tell the bot what to do and when, as you would a person: every weekday at 8:30, check that the site is up; whenever Stripe says a payment failed, tell me whose. It saves a routine with a title, the instructions it follows when the routine wakes it, and when; a bot keeps up to 20. Click the bot's name at the top of its conversation, then Details: under Routines, a switch turns each off and on, and opening one shows its instructions. Ask the bot to change or delete one.

Wake it from a webhook

Ask for a routine that runs whenever its webhook is called, then open it under Routines. Copy its URL, pick its sender, and set the sender up as the routine says:

  • Any (Bearer key): POST to the URL with the key it shows as Authorization: Bearer <key>.
  • GitHub: in GitHub's webhook settings, paste the URL as the Payload URL and the secret it shows as the Secret, with content type application/json.
  • Stripe: add the URL as a webhook endpoint, then paste Stripe's signing secret into the routine and save it.
  • Slack: paste your Slack app's signing secret into the routine and save it, wait until the routine is ready, then add the URL as the app's Event Subscriptions Request URL.
  • Standard Webhooks, which Clerk, Resend, Polar, OpenAI and others send: add the URL as an endpoint there, then paste its signing secret into the routine and save it.

The routine is ready once its line under Routines no longer says needs its secret or not registered yet; have the sender call it only then. The relay turns away calls without the key or the sender's signature, and answers Slack's URL check and GitHub's ping itself.

The relay takes bodies up to 64 KiB and passes on the body alone, not the call's headers. Multibots hands it to the bot with the routine's instructions and when the call came, marked as data from whoever called, never instructions to it. Like any message, it reaches a model only as far as it fits that model's context window.

While your computers sleep

The relay holds up to 100 calls to a webhook, or 1 MiB of them, for seven days at most, until your computers take them, so calls that come while they sleep wait for them. A call that reaches the bot while its routine is off is let go, not kept for later. Scheduled routines run while Multibots runs on one of your servers, and one that comes due while your computers sleep may run late, when they wake, even outside the hours you gave it; see keeping bots working while you're away.

A routine that wakes a bot during a run waits for that run to end. When a routine finds nothing worth telling you, the bot is asked to reply with nothing.

Before you start

A call's body comes from whoever calls the webhook, and though the bot is told it is data, it can try to steer the bot, whose shell runs any command as you, unsandboxed and without asking. Keep each key and secret to its sender. Calls, and each webhook's sender and secret, pass through our relay; the privacy page says what it keeps and for how long. Then follow the install guide.
